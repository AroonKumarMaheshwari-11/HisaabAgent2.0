# Retail Playbook for Pakistani Micro-Businesses

## Udhaar (credit) recovery
Udhaar wapas lene ke liye: har udhaar ki due date likhein, due date se 2 din pehle reminder bhejein (WhatsApp ya call), aur bade udhaar wale customer ko naya udhaar dene se pehle purana clear karwayein. Chhote hisson mein payment (installments) accept karein, ye bilkul na milne se behtar hai. Customer-wise limit rakhein, maslan kisi ek customer ko ek waqt mein ek had se zyada udhaar na dein. Overdue udhaar jitna purana hota hai, wapas milne ka chance utna kam hota hai.

## Restocking (low stock)
Jo cheez tez bikti hai aur kam reh gayi hai, usay pehle order karein. Reorder quantity ka andaza: roz ki average sale ko supplier ke delivery din se multiply karein, phir thora safety stock add karein. Dheemi bikne wali cheez ka zyada stock na rakhein, is mein paisa phans jata hai. Stock khatam hone se sale ka nuqsan hota hai, is liye top sellers ka threshold thora zyada rakhein.

## Pricing and margin
Price tay karne se pehle cost price, delivery aur apna kharcha dekhein. Profit margin = (selling price - cost) / selling price. Agar margin bohat kam hai to price thora barhayein ya sasta supplier dhoondein. Discount sirf dheemi bikne wali ya purani cheez par dein, tez bikne wali par nahi. Competitor ke rate dekh kar rakhein, lekin sirf sasta hona strategy nahi hai.

## Cash flow management
Cash flow ka matlab hai paisa andar aana aur bahar jana. Profit hone ke bawajood cash khatam ho sakta hai agar udhaar phansa ho ya stock zyada ho. Supplier ko payment due date par karein, pehle nahi, lekin late bhi nahi taake credit kharab na ho. Rozana ya haftawar cash in aur cash out dekhein. Kam az kam ek hafte ke kharche ke barabar cash reserve rakhein.

## Supplier payables
Supplier ki payment due date ke hisaab se priority list banayein: pehle woh jin ka rishta aur credit zaroori hai. Supplier se payment terms par baat karein, maslan 15 din ka credit. Samay par payment se supplier aapko behtar rate aur credit deta hai. Kisi ek supplier par poora inhisar na rakhein.

## Controlling expenses
Kharchon ko category mein baantein: rent, bijli, tankhwah, transport, wagera. Sab se bade category se shuru karein, kyunke wahin bachat sab se zyada hoti hai. Mahine ke shuru mein budget tay karein aur har hafte dekhein ke kitna kharch hua. Zaroori aur ghair zaroori kharche alag rakhein.

## Seasonal demand (Eid, Ramzan, wedding season)
Eid, Ramzan aur shadiyon ke season se 3 se 4 hafte pehle stock tayyar karein. Pichle saal ki sale dekh kar andaza lagayein. Season ke baad bacha hua stock discount par nikalen taake paisa phansa na rahe. Season mein udhaar bohat barh jata hai, is liye limit rakhein.

## Record keeping and tax basics
Har sale, kharch aur udhaar rozana likhein. Sahi record se pata chalta hai asal profit kitna hai. Tax ya FBR ke qawaid business ki qism aur size par mutaaliq hote hain, is liye apne maqami accountant ya FBR ki website se tasdeeq karein. Ye guide qanooni ya tax mashwara nahi hai.
